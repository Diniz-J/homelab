# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful. Assume good faith.
- Feedback on the code and the design is welcome; feedback on people
  is not.
- No harassment, slurs, discriminatory jokes or sustained personal
  attacks — in issues, PRs, discussions or any other project space.
- Keep the conversation on the thing you want to change.

## Reporting

Private channels are the right ones:

- Open a private
  [security advisory](https://github.com/Diniz-J/homelab/security/advisories/new)
  with "CoC" in the title and a short account of what happened.
- The maintainer reads it first, nobody else is CC'd, and the
  advisory closes without being published unless there's a reason
  to keep a public record.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may:

1. Edit or remove comments, commits, issues, PRs and other
   contributions.
2. Warn the person privately.
3. Ban the person from project spaces (temporarily or permanently).

Decisions and the reasoning behind them stay private unless the
reported party asks for them to be public.

## Scope

Applies to every project space — GitHub repository, Discussions,
issues, PRs, and any external space the project runs (chats,
events) — and to any interaction that identifies the person as a
project participant.
